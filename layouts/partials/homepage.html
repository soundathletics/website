Welcome to Sound Athletics!

We are a non-profit Special Olympics team serving the Puyallup area. We provide both traditional and limited unified sports opportunities for Bowling (Fall), Powerlifting (Fall), Basketball, (Winter), Cheerleading (Winter), Track & Field (Spring).

Our coaching staff is trained to provide a safe teaching and training environment for our athletes. We welcome athletes of all abilities ages 8 and over, with no pay-to-play requirement.
